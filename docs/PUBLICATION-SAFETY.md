# Publication Safety

## Purpose

I publish the research method for a safe feedback loop, not a private corpus, model, or evaluation ledger.

## Allowed

- Synthetic corpus records.
- Broad descriptions of scale.
- Original gate and quarantine schemas.
- Qualitative evaluation outcomes.
- Candid limitations and unresolved states.

## Excluded

- Private prompts, responses, captures, metrics, or corpus text.
- Live endpoints, accounts, credentials, hardware identities, or local paths.
- Dataset sizes that expose operational scope.
- Model artifacts, private scores, and deployment status.
- Examples that blur holdout and training provenance.

## Project-specific review

Each item must declare synthetic or sanitized provenance. Holdout material stays isolated. Privacy and action safety remain independent promotion gates. Quarantined records cannot be presented as passing.

## Gate

The checker validates JSON, required publication files, Mermaid-only architecture, and common private-data patterns. CI repeats the scan. Human review is still required for new corpus or model claims.
